# SIH26005: 3D CAD Design & Mechanical Layout Specifications

Based on the decision to use rugged **120mm PUF panels** rather than fragile vacuum panels, a complete 3D mechanical layout has been designed.

A fully renderable 3D CAD script (`SIH26005_3D_CAD_Model.scad`) has been included in this project. You can open this file using [OpenSCAD](https://openscad.org/) (a free CAD software) to view, rotate, and export the model to a 3D PNG or STL file for your SIH presentation.

## 1. Core Dimensions (1 Metric Ton Capacity)

*   **Internal Usable Space (Vegetable Storage):**
    *   Width: 2000 mm (2.0 meters)
    *   Length: 2000 mm (2.0 meters)
    *   Height: 2500 mm (2.5 meters)
    *   Total Internal Volume: 10 Cubic Meters ($m^3$)
*   **Insulation:**
    *   Material: Polyurethane Foam (PUF)
    *   Thickness: 120 mm on all 6 sides (Walls, Floor, Roof).
*   **External Footprint (including PUF):**
    *   Width: 2240 mm
    *   Length: 2240 mm
    *   Height: 2740 mm

## 2. Component Placement Strategy (3D Layout)

### A. Phase Change Material (PCM) Flats (The "Ice Blue" Layer)
*   **Location:** Mounted internally on the ceiling (Z-axis offset: 2400mm).
*   **Dimensions:** 1600mm x 1600mm x 80mm thick layer.
*   **Physics Rationale:** Cold air is denser than warm air. By mounting the PCM thermal battery on the ceiling, when the power fails and the PCM begins to melt, the cold air naturally falls (convection) over the vegetables below. This eliminates the need to run an internal circulation fan at night, saving battery power.

### B. Solar PV Array (The "Dark Blue" Layer)
*   **Location:** Mounted on the external roof via brackets.
*   **Orientation:** Tilted at a 15-degree angle.
*   **Dimensions:** Oversized to overhang the structure (creates a shade canopy over the PUF panels, reducing the direct solar heat load on the roof).

### C. External Compressor & Battery Unit (The "Dark Grey" Box)
*   **Location:** Mounted on the exterior right wall.
*   **Dimensions:** 600mm x 1000mm x 1200mm.
*   **Contents:** Houses the BLDC compressor, the condenser coil/fan, the MPPT charge controller, and the 4.8 kWh LiFePO4 battery.
*   **Design Note:** Kept outside the insulated zone to prevent the heat generated by the compressor and electronics from entering the cold room.

### D. The Access Door
*   **Dimensions:** 1000mm Wide x 2000mm High.
*   **Design:** Located off-center to allow maximum unbroken PUF wall space for thermal integrity. Fitted with heavy-duty cam-locks and rubber gaskets to ensure an airtight seal.

## 3. How to use the OpenSCAD file for your Hackathon Pitch:
1. Download and install OpenSCAD.
2. Open `SIH26005_3D_CAD_Model.scad`.
3. Press `F5` to render the 3D model.
4. The PUF walls are programmed to be slightly transparent (`alpha = 0.8`) so you can point out the internal PCM ceiling layer to the judges during your presentation.
5. Go to `File > Export > Export as Image` to save the 3D view for your PowerPoint slides.
